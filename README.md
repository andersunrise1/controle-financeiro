# 💰 DIVISA - Personal Finance Control

**The easiest way to take control of your finances. Simple, fast, and powerful.**

> Stop guessing about your money. DIVISA gives you clarity, insights, and control over every peso you spend.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Most people struggle with personal finance because:
- ❌ Apps are too complicated
- ❌ Can't track where money goes
- ❌ No clear insights on spending patterns
- ❌ Manual tracking is boring & tedious

## ✨ The Solution

**DIVISA** makes personal finance management:
- 📊 Simple dashboard - see all finances at a glance
- 💳 Automatic categorization - AI learns your spending
- 📈 Smart insights - know exactly where your money goes
- 🎯 Budget tracking - set goals and stay on track
- 📱 Mobile-first - manage finances anywhere, anytime
- 🚀 Lightning fast - smooth, responsive interface

---

## 📊 Why DIVISA?

**In Brazil:**
- 💰 +80M adults need better financial control
- 📈 Only 35% use any budgeting tool
- 🎯 Average person saves 15-20% after tracking expenses

**DIVISA targets:** Young professionals, freelancers, small business owners (18-45 years old)

## 🛠 Tech Stack

**Frontend:**
- React / Next.js
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Recharts (data visualization)

**Backend:**
- Node.js / Express
- PostgreSQL
- Redis (caching)

**Features:**
- Real-time expense tracking
- Smart categorization (AI)
- Budget alerts
- Spending analytics
- Multi-account support
- Mobile-responsive UI
